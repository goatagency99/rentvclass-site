About the route

The distance is about 109 km, on the A3 motorway to Ploiești and then the DN1. In normal traffic the drive takes about 2 hours.

Same service to Bușteni and Predeal — prices below.

The car is a Mercedes‑Benz V‑Class with captain's chairs and generous luggage space. Your driver meets you at Arrivals with your name, tracks your flight and drives you straight to your address — hotel, home or office.

FAQ

How much is the transfer from Bucharest Airport to Sinaia?

300 € per car, not per person, for up to 5 passengers with luggage. The price is the same in the other direction, Sinaia to the airport.

How long is the drive from Otopeni to Sinaia?

About 2 hours in normal traffic (~109 km).

Can you carry ski equipment?

Yes, the V‑Class has generous luggage space. Tell us how many bags and how much gear you have when you book.

Do you also drive back to the airport?

Yes, at the same price. We pick you up at your hotel and drive you to Otopeni, timed to your flight.
